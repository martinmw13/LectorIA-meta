# Prior work

Lectoria's first version was built in March 2026 at github.com/martinmw13/lectoria with FastAPI and Vite/React.

This repo is a from-scratch rewrite started 2026-10-27 for the Meta hackathon. It carries over ideas as ideas only: scenes as the unit of text, music matched to emotion with crossfade, Picture this, Picture scene, skip track, and BYOK keys sent only in headers. No code, assets, or data were copied.

Third-party tools in this repo: pstack by Lauren Tan (MIT) and ponytail by DietrichGebert (MIT), vendored in `.agents/skills/` with their licenses.
