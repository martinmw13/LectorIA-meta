## Muse review (round 1)

What this change does: the new `resplit` step cuts weak scenes at strong
sequential emotion shifts and re-classifies the parts. Oz moves from 133
to 151 scenes across 18 splits, all decided by the auto rule. I ran the
full suite (29 files, 252 tests, all pass) and checked the shipped Oz data
with scripts instead of trusting the logs.

Grok Bot items, one line each: 1 agree, both c14s4 parts reclassify as
alegria (0.32 and 0.30). 2 agree, a Jev window throw escapes the per-scene
callback with no catch. 3 agree, classify rewrites provenance without
resplit and resplit has no duplicate-id guard. 4 agree, rejected Muse
answers are never recorded. 5 agree, each part's scene object is built
twice. 6 agree, the USD 0.50 throw runs after all paid calls. 7 agree,
jevRequests increments before checking for a Jev result.

8. **Provenance moods describe the windows, not the stored scenes**
   (Should, `pipeline/resplit.ts`, `data/books/oz/analysis.json`)
   - **What this is:** each split entry records left, right, and margin
     from the window mean vectors used to pick the cut.
   - **Problem:** three splits disagree with the re-classified finals
     stored beside them: c1s1 claims calma to tristeza but the parts are
     suspenso to tristeza; c8s4 claims aventura to alegria but the first
     part is tristeza; c11s5 claims asombro to misterio but the second
     part is tristeza (0.32, misterio second at 0.25).
   - **Fix:** label the fields as window tops or store the final tops
     next to them. Small change, one place.
   - **If we skip it:** anyone reading provenance to explain a split
     repeats moods the scene vectors do not support.

9. **One margin slot per split scene hides later cuts**
   (Should, `pipeline/resplit.ts`)
   - **What this is:** a scene cut twice records only the first boundary
     with one margin, one left/right pair, and one reason; decided_by says
     muse when any cut used Muse.
   - **Problem:** recursion already supports multi-way splits and the
     step test covers two cuts, but the second cut's margin and reason
     never reach provenance. No Oz scene hits this (all 18 split once),
     so this is latent.
   - **Fix:** store one entry per cut instead of one per scene.
   - **If we skip it:** the first three-way split ships provenance that
     describes one cut and hides the other.

10. **Letter suffixes run out past 26 parts** (Nit, `core/resplit.ts`)
    - **What this is:** splitIds adds 97 + i as the suffix letter.
    - **Problem:** a 27-part split would mint `{` instead of a letter.
      Unrealistic on Oz (max is two parts).
    - **Fix:** throw on counts above 26 when touching the function.
    - **If we skip it:** nothing on real data; a pathological input
      mints an odd id.
