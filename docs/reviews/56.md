# Review 56: picture requests follow their own scene

Reviewer: Grok Bot. Written directly, no Muse (mechanical fix from the
architecture review of #54).

Verdict: approved.

## Bugs (since continuous scroll, #53, one hook call follows the scene in view)

1. `storeFigures` closed over the scene that started a figure but applied
   the update to the *current* scene's figures and saved that under the
   starting scene. Scrolling during a ~20 s figure lost the new figure and
   overwrote the starting scene's figures with the current scene's.
2. The scene-sync effect dropped every `loading` figure, so a figure still
   generating vanished when you scrolled back to its scene.
3. Scrolling back to a scene whose scene picture was generating showed the
   idle button instead of the drawing.
4. `busyRef` ignored a second request silently.

## Fix (`app/folio/use-pictures.ts`)

- `storeFigures(sceneId, updater)` reads and writes `figureCache.get(sceneId)`
  directly and calls `setFigures` only when that scene is still current.
- `busyRef` is replaced by a module-level `inFlight` record,
  `{kind:'scene',sceneId} | {kind:'figure',sceneId,id} | null`, released
  by identity in `finally`. Scene sync builds the card and figures from the
  caches plus `inFlight`, so `loading` shows for exactly what is generating.
- A blocked ask shows "One picture at a time" for 2.4 s where it was made:
  the idle card (`SceneCard` idle gains optional `hint`, rendered by
  `ImagePane`), the selection pill label (`popover.hint`), or the failed
  figure's message. Static text, no motion. `book-view.tsx` is unchanged.
- `tryStart` / `finish` / `canStart` in `figure-placement.ts` had no other
  callers and are deleted with their test.
- Out of scope, untouched: the PictureSlot refactor.

## Verification

- New tests in `use-pictures.test.tsx` (jsdom, Node 22): figure finishing
  after a scene switch stays with its scene and leaves B untouched; a
  generating figure shows `loading` on return; a generating scene picture
  shows `loading` on return; a second ask shows the hint and sends no request.
  All four fail on main (A ends up with B's figure; `[]` instead of
  `loading`; `idle` instead of `loading`; no hint) and pass with the fix.
- `vitest run`: 73 files, 627 tests pass. `tsc --noEmit`: clean.
