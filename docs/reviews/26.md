# Review 26: chapter cover, chapter end, panels view

Reviewer: Grok Bot (for Martin). Muse Code: one implementation run (Standard, high, $1.126) and one fix round (Contributor, high, $0.014). No Muse review rounds.

## What it does
- **Cover.** A leading sub-page before each chapter's first scene. It shows the "Chapter" kicker, a big number and the title. The offer card names "Chapter in panels" once, blurs its thumbnails and carries the spoiler line, with "Show me anyway" / "I'll read first" and then "Begin the chapter →".
- **End page.** A trailing sub-page after each chapter's last scene. It shows "End of Chapter N" and the card "Relive this chapter in panels" with an unblurred strip, then "Open the panels", "On to Chapter N+1 →", or "The end." after chapter 24.
- **Panels view.** A modal comic grid with one panel per scene and no numbered circles; a tiny faint number shows in the corner on hover only.
  - Art comes from the session's scene pictures first, then the two bundled prototype panels (c1s2, c1s3), then a hatched placeholder.
  - Captions are at most 90 characters.
  - Only the current chapter's captions reach the client (docs/contracts-chapters.md).
- **Pane pairing.** "Picture this scene | Picture the chapter". The chapter half opens the spoiler gate first ("Show me anyway" / "Not yet").
- **No cost.** No generation, music, server/images or docs/contracts.md changes.
