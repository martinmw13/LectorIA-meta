# Review 41 · Read aloud in the reader

Reviewer: Grok Bot. I ran a local `next build` and `next start`, then drove headless Chrome against `/leer/c3s1` and `/leer/c3s2`.

## What works

- The control renders in the player band with the read-aloud icon. Play starts the chapter audio at the scene span, and pause stops it. The music ducks (its volume dropped from about 0.8 to 0.1) and the rain is untouched. There were no page errors.
- Word sync: the engraving mark (small caps on a hairline) follows the voice word by word, and the sentence tint is applied through `CSS.highlights` (`narration-sentence`). Clicking a word seeks to it, and a 1.5x playbackRate stays in sync.

## Findings (round 1)

1. **Auto page-turn never fires.** Repro: open `/leer/c3s2`, press Read aloud without touching anything else, then seek `audio.currentTime += 33` (or just let it play). The audio reaches paragraphs on sub-page 2, the word mark disappears, and the page stays on "Page 1 of 3" for 9 s or more. Suspects:
   - the follow loop in `read-sync.tsx` reads `span` from the effect closure (stale), not from `live.current`;
   - the lookup matches `pages[].paragraphs[].n` against `chunk.n`, so check that the page and paragraph indices line up with what `onTurnPage` expects;
   - something may stamp `lastManualRef` on mount or on the Read aloud click.
   Add a test that drives the gate with real pages, then fix it. The reader must also reach the next scene, and the next chapter, on its own.
2. **Chapter cover.** On a scene whose first sub-page is the chapter cover (`/leer/c3s1`), Read aloud plays, but the reader stays on the cover with no highlight. When narration starts or reaches text that isn't on screen, it should auto-turn onto the first text page (respecting the manual window).
3. **Engraving mark is wider than the word.** The small-caps overlay (letter-spacing plus weight 600) is wider than the in-flow word, so "bricks." shows as "BRICKS" covering the period. Fit the overlay to the word rect: drop the extra letter-spacing and scale it horizontally to the measured width (`transform: scaleX(w/natural)`, origin left) so it never covers neighbouring characters.
4. **Skin is read once.** `useState(skinTreatment)` reads `data-skin` only on mount. Watch `html[data-skin]` with a MutationObserver so a skin switch restyles the mark live. Martin's rule is to pick by data-skin whenever it is available, with engraving as the default.

## Fixes (round 1)

1. **Auto page-turn.** The tick read `span` (plus `enabled`/`manifest`) from the effect closure while the deps list never re-ran it for a new scene under a kept chapter manifest, so the voice gated against the previous scene's span. Every input now comes from `live.current` each tick, and the gate is the pure `autoTurnTarget()` in `app/narration/turn.ts`, covered with real `paginateScene` pages. Lookup and `textPage` both run in text-page space and `onTurnPage` re-adds the cover shift, so the indices line up. Nothing stamps `lastManualRef` on mount or on the Read aloud click (only pager and figure moves do) — but the stamps used `Date.now()` while the gate reads `performance.now()`, so after any hand move the 8 s window never elapsed; both stamps now use `performance.now()`. Next scene and next chapter advance is the narration controller's `timeupdate`/`ended` handoff across the full scene order; the live-inputs fix keeps the follow loop alive across it.
2. **Chapter cover.** `syncEnabled` no longer requires `kind === "text"`, so the loop runs on the cover (and end page): with no container the mark stays clear, and the first active chunk auto-turns onto its text page through the same manual-window gate. The measure effect also re-runs on text-page change so the mark paints right after a turn.
3. **Engraving mark.** Dropped `letter-spacing: 0.04em` from `.narr-word`; the overlay measures its natural width and shrinks with `transform: scaleX()` (origin left) via pure `engravingScale()`, tested, so it never covers neighbouring characters and never stretches. Colours still come from tokens; no new icons.
4. **Skin.** `useReadSync` watches `html[data-skin]` with a `MutationObserver` and restyles the mark live on switch. The pick rule is unchanged: data-skin whenever available, engraving as the default.

## Fixes (round 2)

Continuous scroll removed sub-pages, covers, and the Begin gate: the whole book is one native-scroll column of `section[data-scene-id]` with whole `<p id data-n>` paragraphs, and the scene most in view is the live scene.

1. **Paging is gone.** Deleted the auto page-turn path (`app/narration/turn.ts` and its test, `onTurnPage`, `lastManual` stamps, cover handling) and the dead `word-map.ts`. `core/reader.ts` already carries no fragment `from` offsets, so `ranges.ts` maps each word straight onto its `p[data-n]` from offset 0. `useReadSync` and `ReadMark` sit in `BookView` with the reading column (`.read-col`, now positioned) as both container and scroller.
2. **Follow.** The spoken paragraph rides in with the existing `followIntoView` (middle band, 4 s pause after user wheel/touch/key); no other scroll code. When follow is paused the voice keeps reading and the mark hides until the reader is back on screen. The sync loop only runs while the audio plays, so nothing animates while paused; under `prefers-reduced-motion` the follow is instant and the mark has no transitions.
3. **Start point and continuity.** Pressing Read aloud starts at the live scene (`useCurrentSceneId` / `getCurrentSceneId`), at that scene's span start, or resumes where it paused when still in that scene. It then plays on through scenes into the next chapter's audio with no user action. A `SceneSync` component registers every live scene with narration; narration never sets the live scene itself, so scroll keeps driving it and auto-follow scrolling moves it.
4. **Highlight per skin.** `highlightStyleFor` now maps the real skin ids: engraving and pencil to engraving, watercolor and chihiro to watercolor, ink and comic to ink-doodle, anything else to engraving. The test covers all six.
5. **Ducking relative to the voice.** The effective music level is min(music, voice × 10^(−20/20)), applied through the existing `duck` multiplier in `Player.applyLevels`, computed from the live music and narration volumes with zero/NaN guards. The rain ambience takes the same duck via a `duck` input on `AmbienceController` that `targetGain()` multiplies in and ramps through the voice. Ramps stay ~400 ms down and ~800 ms up, and the level restores on pause, on end of book, on error, when Read aloud is turned off, and when leaving the reader. The pure math lives in `app/narration/duck.ts` with tests (voice at 1 and 0.5, music already quieter, muted music, zero voice).
6. **Motion and copy.** The Read aloud button is boxless (no border, no filled box) with pointer cursor, token-colour hover/focus-visible change, and a visible focus ring. Copy stays warm English ("Read aloud" / "Pause", "Getting the voice ready…", "The voice isn't available right now"), never "Generated". Clicking a word seeks only while narration is on.
