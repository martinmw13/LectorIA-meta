# Review 41 · Read aloud in the reader

Reviewer: Grok Bot. I ran a local `next build` and `next start`, then drove headless Chrome against `/leer/c3s1` and `/leer/c3s2`.

## What works

- The control renders in the player band with the read-aloud icon. Play starts the chapter audio at the scene span, and pause stops it. The music ducks (its volume dropped from about 0.8 to 0.1) and the rain is untouched. There were no page errors.
- Word sync: the engraving mark (small caps on a hairline) follows the voice word by word, and the sentence tint is applied through `CSS.highlights` (`narration-sentence`). Clicking a word seeks to it, and a 1.5x playbackRate stays in sync.

## Findings (round 1)

1. **Auto page-turn never fires.** Repro: open `/leer/c3s2`, press Read aloud without touching anything else, then seek `audio.currentTime += 33` (or just let it play). The audio reaches paragraphs on sub-page 2, the word mark disappears, and the page stays on "Page 1 of 3" for 9 s or more. Suspects:
   - the follow loop in `read-sync.tsx` reads `span` from the effect closure (stale), not from `live.current`;
   - the lookup matches `pages[].paragraphs[].n` against `chunk.n`, so check that the page and paragraph indices line up with what `onTurnPage` expects;
   - something may stamp `lastManualRef` on mount or on the Read aloud click.
   Add a test that drives the gate with real pages, then fix it. The reader must also reach the next scene, and the next chapter, on its own.
2. **Chapter cover.** On a scene whose first sub-page is the chapter cover (`/leer/c3s1`), Read aloud plays, but the reader stays on the cover with no highlight. When narration starts or reaches text that isn't on screen, it should auto-turn onto the first text page (respecting the manual window).
3. **Engraving mark is wider than the word.** The small-caps overlay (letter-spacing plus weight 600) is wider than the in-flow word, so "bricks." shows as "BRICKS" covering the period. Fit the overlay to the word rect: drop the extra letter-spacing and scale it horizontally to the measured width (`transform: scaleX(w/natural)`, origin left) so it never covers neighbouring characters.
4. **Skin is read once.** `useState(skinTreatment)` reads `data-skin` only on mount. Watch `html[data-skin]` with a MutationObserver so a skin switch restyles the mark live. Martin's rule is to pick by data-skin whenever it is available, with engraving as the default.
