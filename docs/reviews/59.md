# Review 59: narration chapter boundary and click-to-seek

Reviewer: Grok Bot. Implementation by Muse Code (`muse-spark-1.3-contributor`,
one round, 71 calls, about USD 0.04). Muse did not review its own work.

Verdict: approved.

## Bugs (architecture review after #57)

1. A new chapter's first scene could be skipped. `onTime` advanced 0.15 s
   early into the next chapter, the scene effect cleared `advancingRef`
   while that manifest was still fetching, and an `ended` arriving first
   advanced a second time.
2. Clicking a word in another scene misplaced the voice. `read-sync` wrote
   `audio.currentTime` directly: a later scene jumped to that scene's start
   on the next `timeupdate`, an earlier scene lost its highlight to the span
   gate, and drag-selects also sought.

## Fix (`app/narration/use-narration.ts`, `read-sync.tsx`)

- One `advance()` for `timeupdate` and `ended`, gated on
  `cache.get(current.ch)?.key === audio.dataset.key`; a cache miss or stale
  audio does nothing.
- The next chapter's manifest is prefetched on a chapter's last scene. It
  only fills the cache; the current fetch keeps its `loadToken` guard.
- A playing same-chapter advance never seeks (the chapter audio is
  continuous). A chapter that ended mid-scene still seeks to the next span.
- `seekTo(n, t?)` on the controller finds the scene holding paragraph `n`,
  sets it and seeks through `pendingSeek`. `read-sync` only forwards the
  click, returns early on a non-collapsed selection, and seeks to the scene
  start for a paragraph in another chapter (word time unknown until its
  manifest loads).
- Dead `registerScene`, `sameScene` and `setOn` deleted.

## Review notes

- Checked the race by hand: after `advance()` sets the ch2 scene, `ended`
  for ch1 audio hits the key gate (cache miss, or ch2 key vs ch1 audio) and
  returns. Prefetch landing mid-race changes nothing for the same reason.
- `seekScene` consumes `pendingSeek`, so a click wins over the span start
  and the scene effect no longer seeks twice before `playCurrent`.
- Known, harmless: a slow prefetch and the main fetch can both request the
  same manifest once.
