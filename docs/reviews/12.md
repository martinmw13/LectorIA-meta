Findings:

1. Must. The rng call order changed. Before, `evaluateRandom` drew first
   (`pickRandomTrack` called `rng()`) and applied the min-track hold
   afterwards. `selectScheduled` now returns early under the hold, before
   it draws. The visible pick is the same, but a seeded rng now advances
   differently during a hold, and the brief asked for identical rng
   consumption. Fix: draw first, then apply the hold to a pick that is
   not the current track.
2. Should. `canSkip` now comes from the view built at dispatch time with
   the route signal of that moment. Before, it was recomputed on every
   render from `routeSceneId`. Between a route change and the next
   dispatch (sceneEntered or the 1 s tick), the button can be stale for
   up to one tick. Either confirm sceneEntered always follows a route
   change in the same effect pass, or note the bound in the contracts doc.
